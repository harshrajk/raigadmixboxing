RMBA OBJECTIVES

Be the representative authority with all other recognized bodies responsible for Olympic, Asian and Continental Games.
Promote the practice of sport and improve the standard of performance in the spirit of fair play.
Co-operate with public authorities and private enterprises in the promotion of sport.
Ensure regular celebration of Games every four years.
Oppose any political or commercial abuse of sport and athletes.
Encourage and support athletes' future and health.
Fight against doping and prohibited substances.
