The Raigad Mix Boxing Association (RMBA) was built to make combat sports training accessible to every student, regardless of background. Founded by Mr. Rakesh Suresh Mhaskar in Dhatav, Roha, RMBA combined discipline from boxing, kick boxing, and martial arts into one structured mix boxing system.

Through sustained coaching, district-level initiatives, and federation-level support, the sport expanded across Maharashtra and then to multiple states in India. RMBA leadership focuses on athlete development, fair governance, self-defense awareness, and long-term opportunities for youth.

Today, the administration team continues to strengthen operations, support tournaments, and maintain standards so members receive consistent training pathways from grassroots to national and international participation.
