Grand Master Rakesh Suresh Mhaskar is the Founder and General Secretary of the Indian Mix Boxing Federation and the visionary behind the development of Mix Boxing in India.

Born on May 06, 1974 in Mumbai, Maharashtra, he belongs to a humble farming family from Dhatav village in Roha Taluka, Raigad District.

Through dedication to martial arts, he developed Mix Boxing by combining Karate, Kickboxing, Thai Boxing and various self-defense systems.
